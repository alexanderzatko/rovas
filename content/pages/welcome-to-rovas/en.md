<p>Rovas allows you to participate in <a href="https://neofund.sk/" target="_new">NEO</a> - a community economic system with simple rules:</p>

<ul>
	<li>create <strong>any</strong> product or service that can be rewarded in or purchased through Rovas and you will get 10 <strong>chrons</strong> (the NEO currency) for every hourt of your labor,</li>
	<li>if somebody buys, or rewards your product or service, Rovas will give you a non-monetary <strong>merit</strong> reward,</li>
	<li>use your chrons to buy other people's products, or exchange it for any other currency.</li>
</ul>

<p>See the <strong><a href="/rules">Rules</a></strong> page for more information</p>
