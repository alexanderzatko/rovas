Rovas allows you to participate in <a href="https://neofund.sk/" target="_new">NEO</a> - a community economic system with simple rules:

- create **any** product or service that can be rewarded in or purchased through Rovas and you will get 10 **chrons** (the NEO currency) for every hourt of your labor,
- if somebody buys, or rewards your product or service, Rovas will give you a non-monetary **merit** reward,
- use your chrons to buy other people's products, or exchange it for any other currency.

See the **[Rules](/rules)** page for more information
